The i in purple

The a and the i share one stem. Now the whole i, stem and dot, carries the colour, so the word reads as a ink bowl leaning on a lit i, followed by the m. Four purples, from cool violet to warm grape.

The two tiles at the end of each row are the app icon options: ink tile with the purple i, or purple tile with a white i and a softened bowl.
