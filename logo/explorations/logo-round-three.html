aim · identity, round three

Twelve new directions

A wider spread this time: merging shapes, overlapping timelines, letter monograms, app tiles and a terminal wordmark. Each still uses one ink colour and one accent for the point where two pieces of work meet.

aim

Merge N1

Two sessions flowing into each other, ink becoming accent. Soft and organic; says sync without any arrows.

Most expressive · gradient needs a flat fallback for tiny sizes

aim

Lanes N2

Two sessions as timeline bars; the dashed frame marks the stretch where they overlap. Very close to what aim actually computes.

Most literal to the product · busy at 16 px

aim

Peak N3

Two paths climbing toward the same point, with the accent waiting at the top. Doubles as a sharp, quiet A.

Strong silhouette · reads as A and as convergence

aim

Aperture N4

An eye that notices for you. aim watches the team's work so you don't have to. Simple and memorable.

Memorable · might read as surveillance, a risk for a privacy-sensitive tool

aim

Talk N5

Two speech bubbles overlapping: you and the other session in conversation. Leans into the peer agent.

Friendly · bubbles are a crowded category

aim

Bowl N6

A lowercase a whose bowl is a scope with the accent at its centre. Takes the F idea into the first letter instead of the i.

Monogram · works as an app icon and inside the wordmark

Prompt N7

A terminal prompt with an accent cursor, on a solid tile. Pure Claude Code territory; the wordmark is set in mono with the same cursor.

Most developer-native · less specific to aim's idea

aim

Tile i N8

A solid tile with the i cut out and its dot lit. The cleanest app-store style icon of the set.

Cleanest icon · relies on the wordmark for meaning

aim

Halves N9

Two half-circles slightly out of step, about to lock into one. Your work and theirs, almost in sync.

Abstract · bold at any size

aim

Orbit N10

A teammate's session circling the work you share. Implies a system that keeps track of everyone.

Space-y · common shape in tech logos

aim

Windows N11

Two session windows stacked; the overlap becomes a leaf of accent. A sharper, more structured take on the overlap idea.

Clear meaning · the leaf is a distinctive detail

ai ligature N12

The a and the i share one stem, so aim reads as a, i, m with only two letters drawn before the m. The lit dot sits on the shared stem.

Ownable wordmark trick · hints at AI without saying it

Pick the ones that feel closest, even loosely, and say what you dislike about the rest (too thin, too literal, too techy, the blue). That tells me more than another round of guesses.
